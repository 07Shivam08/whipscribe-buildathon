# Starter prompt — Track 1, find what is wrong with the product

Paste this as the first message to Claude, Codex, Gemini, Cursor or any other tool.

---

Read AGENTS.md in this repository first and follow it.

I am doing Track 1 of the WhipScribe Buildathon: finding UI and UX problems on
https://whipscribe.com on mobile and desktop, and proposing fixes.

Help me work like a careful reviewer:

1. Give me a walkthrough plan: the flows a real person goes through (arrive,
   try a free tool, sign in, upload or paste a link, wait, read the transcript,
   use the library, look at credits and pricing), and for each flow what to
   watch for on a phone versus a laptop.
2. As I describe what I see, turn each problem into one issue using the
   *UI bug* template in `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/ui-bug.md`: device and browser,
   steps, expected, actual, how much it matters. Keep each to the point.
3. For the problems that matter most, draft a *Proposal* using
   `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/proposal.md`: what to change, why, and a fix that is
   smaller than the problem. Ask me for a screenshot or sketch where one is
   needed.
4. Do not guess what the product does; I will tell you what I see. Do not
   invent bugs.

Start with the walkthrough plan.
